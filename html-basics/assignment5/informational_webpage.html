Welcome to Manali

Manali is a beautiful hill station located in Himachal Pradesh, India. It is surrounded by snow-covered mountains, green forests and beautiful valleys.

The destination is popular among tourists who enjoy nature, adventure and peaceful surroundings.

Famous Places

Manali has many beautiful places to visit such as Solang Valley, Rohtang Pass, Hidimba Temple and Mall Road.

Solang Valley is popular for adventure activities such as paragliding and skiing during suitable seasons.

Culture of Manali

The culture of Manali reflects the traditions of Himachal Pradesh. Local festivals, traditional clothing, music and handicrafts are important parts of the region.

Local Food

Visitors can enjoy traditional Himachali dishes such as Siddu, Dham, Madra and various local breads and beverages.
